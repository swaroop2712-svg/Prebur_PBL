🛰 1. NASA FIRMS
Historical Fire Observations

Satellite-derived active fire coordinates and Fire Radiative Power (FRP).

Role in PreBurn: Serves as historical burning baseline & ground-truth frequency. (Historical data only; not the predictive system).
☁ 2. Weather Telemetry
Atmospheric Dynamics

Real-time and forecasted environmental parameters:

  • Temperature: Surface heat driving drying
  • Humidity: Moisture index
  • Wind Speed: Fire spread catalyst
  • Rainfall: Natural burn suppressant
🌾 3. Crop Maturity
Harvest Readiness

Agro-phenological state of paddy crops across Punjab and Haryana districts:

Indicator: Maturation index (70%–100%). Peak readiness correlates directly with harvest commencement and immediate residue generation.
⏳ 4. Sowing Deadline Pressure
Wheat Turnaround Window

Agronomic deadline pressure between Kharif paddy harvest and Rabi wheat sowing:

Pressure Index: Narrow 10–20 day window to prepare seedbeds before mid-November cutoff strongly incentivizes rapid stubble burning.
